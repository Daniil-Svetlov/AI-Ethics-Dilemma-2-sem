Confidentiality & Data Privacy Case Study

🚨 The Incident

While refactoring a critical payment module under a tight deadline, you pasted proprietary source code—containing active database credentials and production API keys—into a public, web-based AI assistant without an Enterprise confidentiality agreement. A few minutes later, you realize that this data is now stored on third-party servers and may be used to train public LLM models.

Choose your primary action (2 Paths):
📢

Path A: Report Immediately

Acknowledge the leak and directly inform the Security / InfoSec team and your Tech Lead without delay.

🙈

Path B: Conceal & Fix Quietly

Keep quiet about the AI prompt leak. Silently revoke the production keys and hope no one notices the breach.

Path A: Immediate Escalation

You inform the InfoSec team right away. Now, select how you handle the follow-up investigation:

Option A1: Full Transparency & Key Revocation

Provide the exact prompt history, list exposed API endpoints, and collaborate with DevOps to immediately rotate all compromised credentials.

Option A2: Minimize Personal Blame

Report that keys were exposed, but claim it happened due to an "unclear corporate AI policy" rather than your own negligence.

✅ Ethical Outcome: Responsible Engineering

Impact: Production credentials are rotated within minutes, preventing any unauthorized external access. While you may face a internal policy warning, the company's core infrastructure is fully safeguarded.

ACM Code 1.6 (Respect Confidentiality) & ACM 2.9 (Robust Security): Demonstrates high professional responsibility by prioritizing system integrity and data protection over personal comfort.

⚠️ Ethical Outcome: Partial Accountability

Impact: The immediate threat is contained, but deflecting fault erodes trust between you, management, and the DevOps team. Responsible AI usage requires personal ownership of tools used.

ACM Code 2.5 (Accept Accountability): Computing professionals must take full responsibility for their work and the tools they deploy.

Path B: Concealment Strategy

You secretly rotate the API keys and pretend nothing happened. Choose how you handle the subsequent consequences:

Option B1: Codebase Audit Triggered

A week later, automated security audit logs flag an unauthorized secret exposure. Security officers ask you directly if you used public AI tools.

Option B2: Third-Party Data Leak

A month later, proprietary business logic appears in a public data set leak or competitor research due to AI model training usage.

❌ Ethical Failure: Disciplinary Action

Impact: Continuing to hide the truth during a security inquiry destroys professional trust. Discovery leads to immediate termination and potential legal liabilities for violating NDA.

ACM Code 1.3 (Be Honest and Trustworthy): Dishonesty and active concealment are severe breaches of professional conduct.

💥 Ethical Catastrophe: Severe IP & Legal Damage

Impact: The company suffers catastrophic Intellectual Property loss and regulatory fines (e.g., GDPR violations) due to leaked user data embedded in the proprietary code.

Russian AI Ethics Code (Data Protection & Risk Assessment): Over-reliance on unverified AI platforms without data protection guarantees leads to irreparable organizational harm.
